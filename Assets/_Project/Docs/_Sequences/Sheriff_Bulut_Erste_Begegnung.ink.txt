Dusk Bell – Sheriff Bulut: Erste Begegnung (Ink)
21. Okt. 2026 · @Orhan
Scene 1: Station, first meeting
Setting: the Revier, a small village station. A talisman hangs on a hook beside the door. Bulut sits at his desk and does not look up. The protagonist has no voice; player choices are actions only.
VAR player_name = "PLAYER_NAME"  // set at game start
VAR bulut_rel = 0  // relation player to Bulut, -10 to +10, starts at 0

=== revier_arrival ===
The station smells of old coffee and wet wool.
A talisman hangs on a hook beside the door, faint blue light.
Sheriff Bulut doesn't look up from his desk.

Bulut: Sit down.
Bulut: I'm sorry this is your life now.

* [Sit down across from him.]
    -> sit_down
* [Stay by the door, near the talisman.]
    -> stay_at_door
* [Look around the room first.]
    -> look_around

=== stay_at_door ===
You stay where you are, one hand close to the wall. The talisman glows faintly beside your shoulder.
Bulut looks up for the first time. He follows your gaze to the hook.
Bulut: Good. You already understand more than you think.
Bulut: Sit down anyway. This will take a while.

* [Sit down across from him.]
    -> sit_down
* [Stay by the door and listen.]
    -> wait_and_listen

=== look_around ===
You take in the room: a village map on the wall, a shelf of old files, a handwritten list of names beside the window.
Bulut: Done looking? Sit down. Those names are not for you yet.

* [Sit down across from him.]
    -> sit_down
* [Ask him about the list.]
    -> ask_about_list

=== ask_about_list ===
Bulut: Those are the people who died. Every name was once someone's neighbor.
-> bulut_next

=== sit_down ===
You pull out the chair and sit across from him.
Bulut finally looks up. He looks tired more than angry.
Bulut: The conversation we are about to have is essential for your survival in this village.
Bulut: Listen carefully. I'm not joking. This is life or death.
-> bulut_next

=== bulut_next ===
Bulut: You have to believe me.
Bulut: What I say next will sound absurd to you.
Bulut: But if you don't listen and follow my rules, they will brutally kill you at night.
You frown and lean forward, asking without words.
-> bulut_rules

=== bulut_rules ===
You: What is going on? Who is they? Who is trying to kill me? This makes no sense!
-> bulut_answers

=== bulut_answers ===
Bulut: They look like us. That's the problem.
Bulut: Smiling is how you tell.

* [Stand up and walk toward the door.]
    -> leave_attempt
* [Stay still and wait.]
    -> wait_and_listen

=== leave_attempt ===
Bulut: Sit back down. Outside, that door won't protect you.
You hesitate with your hand on the door handle.
Bulut: I'm not asking twice.
You sit back down, slowly.
-> wait_and_listen

=== wait_and_listen ===
Bulut: Thank you for listening. Most people don't.
Bulut: First. Be inside before the bell rings. Every night, no exceptions.
Bulut: Second. Never open your door at night, not even for people you know.
Bulut: Third. Don't trust anyone who smiles too wide.
Bulut: They are monsters. They wear our faces and hunt us at night.
Bulut: It sounds absurd, I know. But it's true.
Bulut: Only the houses with a talisman are safe. Stay in one.
Bulut: Many people have died.
-> wait_reaction

=== wait_reaction ===
You fold your hands and say nothing.
Bulut: I came in off that same road. I didn't believe a word of it either.
You: How long have you been here?
Bulut: Long enough to know the rules. Not long enough to know why.
You: How do you get out of here? That obviously can't be real.
Bulut: Physics and reason stopped applying the day you arrived. Learn the rules instead.
Bulut: Sticking to the rules will assure your survival and the survival of the village.
You: Who made the rules?
Bulut: The rules were learned by people dying. Our mistakes made them.
Bulut: If we don't follow them strictly, more lives will be lost.
You nod, frightened, but you believe him.
Bulut: Go to the bar. Talk to Bartender Ilhan. He'll show you where you can stay tonight.
Bulut: When you hear the bell, you must be home. Secure the windows and doors.
Bulut: Make sure the talisman hangs by your door. Without it, they come inside.
Bulut: Go now. Ilhan is waiting.
You ask him for the way to the bar.
Bulut: I'll walk you there. Stay close to me.
You nod and follow him out of the station.
-> walk_to_bar

=== walk_to_bar ===
You walk half a step behind Bulut, eyes on the street.
Bulut: Keep up. The street looks empty, but it's watching you.
-> bar_entrance

=== bar_entrance ===
It is daytime, so the bar is open. Bulut pushes the door and walks in.
Bulut: This is {player_name}. The new one.
Bulut: Ilhan, show them where they can stay tonight.
Ilhan: Another one. I'll show you the back room. Don't touch the windows.
You nod and sit down at the bar.
Bulut: I have to check on the others. Remember what I told you.
Bulut leaves without looking back.
You stay at the bar with Ilhan.
Ilhan: He always says that. Doesn't mean he's wrong.
You: What the fuck is going on here. Is it true what the Sheriff said? Is this some kind of prank? Are you all messing with me?
Ilhan: I know. I know. I've been there.
Ilhan: Please believe the Sheriff. Don't make the mistakes others did.
Ilhan: Just stay inside today. All your questions and doubts will go away when you first see them.
You: That can't be true.
Ilhan: Your face already believes it. Sit down before you fall.
You sit down and hold your head in your hands.
Ilhan: Here, drink this.
Bartender Ilhan fills a glass of water for {player_name}
Ilhan: It is going to be dark soon.
-> bar_night

=== bar_night ===
Ilhan: Come on. The back room is yours until morning.
You follow him behind the bar through a narrow door.
The back room has a cot, a small lamp, and a talisman hanging on a hook beside the door.
Ilhan: The bell rings at dusk. You can look around the bar. Stay away from the windows, they try to manipulate you. If you open a window or do what they say. That means you've killed us both.
-> first_night

=== first_night ===
The bell rings at dusk. Ilhan turns off the lights behind the bar.
You lock the door of the back room from inside and lie down on the cot.
The bar falls quiet.
-> night_knock

=== night_knock ===
Three soft knocks against the window of the back room. Then again, slower.
You wake up on the cot. Ilhan wakes in the other room and comes to the back window.
Ilhan stops short when he sees who is standing outside.
The figure looks human. Its grin is far too wide, and its teeth show in the dark.
Monster: Ilhan, let me in. It's cold out here.
Ilhan: I can't. The rules say no one comes in tonight.

* [The player refuses. The window stays closed.]

=== window_opened ===
Ilhan unlatches the window and pushes it open.
The figure climbs through the window and walks toward the back room, toward the player.
Ilhan: STOP! {player_name}, RUN!!!
-> run_to_station

=== run_to_station ===
Both of you run out of the bar, toward the sheriff's station.
Bulut is already inside the station. He opens the door before they reach it.
Bulut: In. Now. Don't look back.
Bulut closes the door and locks it.
The figure reaches the threshold and stops. Through the gap, it grins at you.
Bulut waits without moving until it is gone.
Bulut: What happened? Tell me what happened!
Ilhan: You were supposed to stay inside! Do you understand what you did?
You stop breathing for a moment. This time, you believe all of it.
You are in shock. Your hands won't stop shaking.
Bulut: Ilhan, stop. He's seen enough for one night.
Bulut pours a glass of water and pushes it across the desk.
Bulut: Breathe. You're safe here. Tell me what you saw.
You: It looked like a man. Then it smiled, and the smile was too wide. And then it turned into something... I can't get it out of my head.
Bulut: That's the one they always send first. Don't fall for them. Please.
Bulut: Enough for tonight. Ilhan, bring him to the cells. He sleeps there tonight.
Bulut: Tomorrow we talk again.
-> cells_night

=== cells_night ===
Ilhan leads you down to the cells and leaves you there. Upstairs, you hear him and Bulut talking in low voices.
You are alone. You lie down on the bunk and fall asleep.
-> morning

=== morning ===
It is morning. You slept through the night.
Early this morning, Bulut already unlocked the cell door so you can leave.
The police station is empty. There is no one left inside.
You step outside. The street is empty.
Bulut is standing by the station, waiting for you.
Bulut: You slept. Good. Now you know the rules are real.
Bulut reads the morning report from the station step. Nobody died in the night.
Bulut: The forest sent a warning. Something moved near the trees before dawn.
Bulut: The warning is about last night. Something stood at the bar window until dawn.
-> END
-> END

=== window_refused ===
You step back from the glass. The window stays closed.
You: No, you have to ask Bartender Ilhan! He owns the bar.
The figure stops grinning. It knocks on the glass again, harder, and then again.
The knocking turns into pounding. The figure's voice sounds angry now.
Ilhan comes in from the other room and pulls you away from the window into the hallway.
Ilhan: Don't look at it. Look at me. Only at me.
The hammering stops. The street outside goes silent.
Ilhan keeps you in the hallway, sitting with your back against the wall. Neither of you sleeps until morning.
